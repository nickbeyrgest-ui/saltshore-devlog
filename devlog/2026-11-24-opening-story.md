# November 24, 2026 — The opening journey takes shape

**English** · [Русский](2026-11-24-opening-story.ru.md)

Saltshore's first playable story sequence has taken a step forward. Mira awakens after the shipwreck, explores the shore and its remains, ventures into a cave, discovers a spring, and can take a rest by the fire.

These early events now form a more connected introduction, with opportunities to explore and gather useful resources.

**Current focus:** polishing the presentation, expanding story interactions, and making the opening feel like one cohesive journey.

**Release:** The first public version is targeted for December 31, 2026, subject to testing and completion.
