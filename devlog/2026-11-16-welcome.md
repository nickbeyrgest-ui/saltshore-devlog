# November 16, 2026 — Welcome to the Saltshore devlog

**Language:** **English** · [Русский](2026-11-16-welcome.ru.md)

Welcome to the development journal for **Saltshore / Соль на ладонях**, an independent, adult-oriented, story-driven RPG.

After a shipwreck, Mira finds herself on an unfamiliar island. She must explore, gather resources, meet its inhabitants, and make choices that will shape what comes next.

## Why this journal exists

- To share verified progress without inflated promises.
- To show how the game's systems and creative direction evolve.
- To publish approved screenshots and, eventually, tested public builds.
- To keep a development history players can follow.

## How Saltshore is made

The game uses RPG Maker MZ. The creator also works with ChatGPT, OpenAI Codex, OpenCode, ComfyUI, and local/open-weight AI models. These tools assist with planning, coding, testing, documentation, and visual experiments. The creator retains control over game design, art direction, and final approval.

**The game is intended to be free.** Optional support will not unlock essential story content or give players gameplay advantages.

## Where things stand

Saltshore has an internal technical prototype with an opening route and several working systems. There is no public build yet, and the official website is still being prepared.

Future posts will cover actual milestones, new approved visuals, and verified progress.
